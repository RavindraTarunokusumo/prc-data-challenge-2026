# Hypothesis H020 v1: CatBoost (ordered target statistics) against LightGBM, on the H018 configuration

Batch X-D04-S01-0001 (see `H018_v1.md`). Chain step 3.

**Provenance (INC-0005).** `gbm.catboost`, `routed.routed_catboost` (commit `e9120a9`) and the first calibration were implemented by a `claude-sonnet-5-5` worker to the researcher's specification, and the researcher reviewed them. The `max_ctr_complexity` addendum is the researcher's.

## Research Question

Does CatBoost's native handling of high-cardinality categoricals improve on the champion's LightGBM, with the same features, the same training rows and the same routing?
- CatBoost uses ordered target statistics and ordered boosting.
- This is the Day 4 alternative to explicit priors (H019): the learner builds its own out-of-fold category statistics.

## Observation

- **High-cardinality categoricals in FS2** (after FS1's `__RARE__` collapse, R3 training rows): `stand` 1,343, `ades` 606, `op_prefix` 523, `actype` 120, `airport_runway` 69.
- **LightGBM** sorts categories by gradient statistics at each split, with a per-split limit. **CatBoost** replaces each category with an ordered, out-of-sample target statistic (Prokhorenkova et al., 2018).
- **Calibration** (permuted target, R3, no metric; `research/day-04/eda/catboost_calibration*.json`):
  - With CatBoost's default categorical feature combinations, per-iteration cost grows with iterations (0.45 → 1.1 s per iteration), so 1,000 iterations exceed CLASS-L.
  - **With `max_ctr_complexity: 1`** (no combinations), cost is linear at 0.29 s per iteration and RAM is flat at 3.97 GB.

## Mechanism

- CatBoost's ordered target statistics give each high-cardinality level a leakage-controlled, smoothed target encoding at every split. This is the prior mechanism of H019, learned inside the model.
- It could do better than LightGBM's gradient-sorted categorical splits on sparse levels (stands, operators, destinations).
- **Against it:**
  - symmetric (oblivious) trees of depth 6 are far less flexible per tree than 255-leaf LightGBM trees;
  - disabling combinations removes CatBoost's automatic stand × runway interaction, the pair H019 targets explicitly.

## Proposed Change

**Model:** `routed_catboost` (the same routing and `route_train_exclude: true` as H018; the LIRF NM-missing validation rows take E005's ridge). **Features:** FS2.

**Tier 1 parameters, fixed a priori** (one configuration, no search):
