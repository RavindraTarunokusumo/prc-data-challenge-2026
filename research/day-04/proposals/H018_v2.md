## Research Question

E019 (the champion) trains its LightGBM on every training row, including the LIRF NM-missing rows it never serves, because routing acts only at prediction.
- **Does removing those rows from the LightGBM's training set remove the out-of-range predictions on non-LIRF NM-missing rows (D3-C2)?**
- **What does it cost or gain on all rows?**

## Mechanism

- **Squared loss weights the convention rows far beyond their count.** One LIRF convention row with y ≈ 6,000 s has the squared-error weight of hundreds of ordinary rows.
- **Leaves fitted to them are selected by inputs that grow with the schedule delay.**
  - `d_sched` (M3) and the SCHED-anchored T windows: the t_off proxy is `coalesce(AOBT_3, EOBT_1, SCHED)`, so an NM-missing row's in-taxi window can start at SCHED.
  - In E017 the leaves use `d_sched` alone. In E019 the T counts give the same signal at finer resolution (D3-C2).
- **Non-LIRF NM-missing rows with a long schedule delay land in those leaves.** They get large positive predictions, or large negative ones where later trees correct the residual of neighbouring rows.
- **Routing never serves the LIRF rows' predictions,** so training on them only exports the convention to rows that do not follow it.
- **Removing them from the LightGBM's training set cuts the source, and changes nothing else in the design:**
  - features are computed on the whole masked view first, so no other row's congestion features change;
  - the ridge still trains on every row;
  - routing is unchanged.

## Proposed Change

- **E019's configuration with a single parameter:** `route_train_exclude: true` (`src/prc/models/routed.py`, commit `7ea6b55`).
  - The LightGBM's training set becomes the fold's training rows minus `ADEP_mvt == LIRF & flt_missing == 1`.
  - Everything else is identical: FS2, parameters, deterministic procedure, and the routed ridge fitted on every training row.
- **Test:** `tests/test_models.py::test_routed_train_exclude_drops_only_routed_training_rows`.
  - With the flag set, non-routed rows equal a LightGBM fitted on the frame minus the routed training rows, and routed rows equal the ridge.
  - Absent or false, the predictions are byte-identical to the Day 3 procedure.

## Falsification Criterion

**Tools.** These are frozen from the X-D04-S01-0002 submission commit until the chain completes:

- `gbm.py`, `linear.py` and `models/routed.py`;
- the `fs0` to `fs2` bodies of `features.py` and `congestion.py`.

**What H018 claims.**
- **D3-C2 as recorded:** the congestion block's out-of-range increment on `NM_missing_other` is the LIRF convention learned from LIRF NM-missing training rows and carried by SCHED-anchored T windows. That increment is +63 of +68 in the > 3 h band, against the matched no-T reference E021.
- **The treatment removes that increment, and improves the treated subgroup's accuracy.**
- **Not claimed:** that LIRF NM-missing training rows drive the out-of-range predictions of LightGBM fits without T windows (the < 1 h band). That would need an FS1 or FS2_P fit with the same exclusion, which is not proposed.

### Falsification clauses (under B2, either one bars promotion)

1. **The > 3 h band.** H018's `NM_missing_other` out-of-range count (< 0 s or > 3,600 s on bulk rows) in the **> 3 h `d_sched` band**, summed over the 5 development folds (`range_check.py <H018> --bands`), is **> 50**.
   - **Why 50.** It is the midpoint between the matched no-T reference E021 (18) and E019 (81). It lies above every fit without T windows (13–41), and about two times the largest shift in this band (seed +15) from each hypothesis's prediction.
   - **≤ 50** reads: the increment is removed, consistent with D3-C2.
   - **> 50** reads: SCHED-anchored T windows produce the increment without the LIRF NM-missing training rows. D3-C2's attribution to those rows is falsified.
2. **Accuracy on the treated subgroup** (unchanged). `compare.py <H018> E019` gives `NM_missing_other.delta_rmse_bulk` ≥ 0 on **two or more** of R1, R2, R3 and W1. These are the four folds where E019 lost to E017 there, by +77 to +234 s.

### Integrity clause

## Alternative Explanations

- **Clause 1 met (> 3 h band > 50).** SCHED-anchored T windows extrapolate on long-delay NM-missing rows without the LIRF convention rows: sparse support above 3 h (528 bulk rows over 5 folds) is enough. D3-C2's attribution is then wrong, and a treatment would have to act on the T windows, not on the training set.
- **Clause 1 not met but clause 2 met.** The > 3 h increment is removed without an accuracy gain on the subgroup. The out-of-range predictions would then be a symptom that shares a cause with, but does not carry, the subgroup's error.
- **Both clauses not met, total still about 100.** This is the expected case. The increment is removed, and the < 1 h generic phenomenon remains. It is not explained by D3-C2 and not addressed by H018.
