# Hypothesis H019 v1: fold-local historical priors (FS3) on the H018 configuration

## Research Question

Do fold-local target statistics over static keys reduce error beyond the static keys themselves (FS1) and congestion (FS2), in the same learner? The main key is stand × runway, a taxi-route proxy. This is the brief's Day 4 question (§11).

## Observation

**EDA:** `research/day-04/eda/priors_summary.md` and `priors.json`.
- **Target hygiene.** Targets were read only for the never-validation months 2025-01, 03, 04, 05, 06 and 08. Priors were fitted on {01, 03, 04, 05} and evaluated on {06, 08}, on NM-present bulk rows (368,958 rows).
- **Incremental R² over an airport × local-hour prior** (cross-fitted linear model):

|---|---|---|

  - Stability is the correlation of airport-demeaned key means fitted on {01, 03} against {04, 05}.
  - **K5 subsumes K4:** [K1, K5] gives 0.4505 and [K1, K4, K5] gives 0.4504.
  - K3, K6, K7 and K9 together add +0.007 on top of [K1, K5].
  - The mean works as well as the median or q20. The low-quantile "unimpeded" proxy adds nothing over the mean.
- **Coverage.** The share of rows whose key has at least 30 training rows is at least 0.95 for K5 on every validation and ranking month. K5 coverage is 0.968 for January 2026 and 0.958 for July 2026, target-free.
- **Why a GBM may not already have this signal.** In FS1, stand levels under 100 training rows collapse to `__RARE__`. The stand × runway interaction has about 12,000 levels. LightGBM's categorical splits are limited per split (`max_cat_threshold`, default 32). A 255-leaf tree can approximate the interaction only partially.

## Mechanism

- **Taxi-out time decomposes into an unimpeded component and a congestion component.**
  - The unimpeded component is set mainly by the stand-to-runway route: distance and taxiway layout.
  - This is the basis of the PRU "additional taxi-out time" indicator, which measures delay against an unimpeded reference per stand–runway group.
- **FS2 carries congestion (Day 3) and the stand and runway identities separately (FS1),** but not their joint route effect at the stand × runway resolution.
- **A smoothed historical mean of the target per stand × runway pair** supplies the route effect directly, with a count feature so the learner knows how much to trust it.
- **Airport-level priors on operator and aircraft type** supply the remaining static structure at raw (uncollapsed) resolution.

## Proposed Change

**FS3 = FS2 + 5 columns** (`src/prc/priors.py`, `7ae934f`):
- `pr_stand_rwy`, `pr_stand_rwy_logn`, `pr_rwy_hour`, `pr_op`, `pr_actype`.
- Each is a smoothed mean (m-estimate, M = 50, fixed a priori; no tuning) toward a parent in a hierarchy: K5 → runway → airport; K3 → runway; K6, K7 → airport.
- **Source rows:** the fold's training rows with NM present and y < 3,600 s.
- **Out-of-fold within training: leave-one-month-out (LOMO).**
  - A training row's priors use other training months only.
  - Validation rows use all training months.

**Model:** H018's exact configuration (routed LightGBM, `route_train_exclude: true`, E019's parameters). Only `feature_set: FS3` changes.

## Controlled Variables

## Leakage Analysis

- **Target leakage:**
  - A training row's features never contain its own target, or any target from its own calendar month (LOMO; test 1 in `tests/test_priors.py`).
  - Validation targets are never read: they are null in the masked view, and the function ignores them even if filled (test 2).
  - The source filter on `flt_missing` and y < 3,600 uses training rows only (test 3).
- **Temporal leakage:**
  - In the non-causal folds S1 and W1, priors may use training months later than the validation month. The R folds are causal. This is the frozen fold design; the causal twins S1c and W1c bound it.
  - **Cross-month:** at ranking, each SUBMIT fold contains one ranking month with null targets, so priors come from 2025 only.
- **Availability:** the keys (stand, runway, operator prefix, aircraft type, scheduled local hour) are FS0/FS1 columns already admitted (label P).

## Alternative Explanations

- **A gain from LOMO priors could be a smoothing effect,** a regulariser for stand levels rather than route information. K5 dominating K4 in the EDA argues for route information, but H019 does not separate the two.
- **A null result could mean** the GBM already represents stand × runway through FS1's keys. The Day 2 M1 result (static keys ≈ −7 s) is consistent with that.
