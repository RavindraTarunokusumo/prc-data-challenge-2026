# Hypothesis H013: seed-invariant LightGBM on FS1 (H009 without row and feature subsampling)

Day 2 batch X-D02-S01-0004: **H013** (candidate) and **H014** (its matched M1 reference). Context: `experiments/E015/analysis.md`, `research/STATE.md`.

## Research Question

Can the H009 model be made reproducible under the frozen criterion 6 by removing its only seed-consuming components (row bagging and feature subsampling), without losing its mechanisms or its margin over the champion?

## Observation

- **H009 v3 (E012) was not falsified** on any of its four clauses. Against E005 it gave −106.58 s, 7/7 WIN, and the criterion 8 LIRF objection is resolved.
- **Its reproduction (E015, seed 43) failed criterion 6:** |Δ| was 1.73 s on R3 and 3.50 s on S1, against a 1.0 s tolerance. H009 is INCONCLUSIVE.
- **The seed variance is localised** (`E015_vs_E012_mech_*.json`):

  | Population | R3 \|Δ\| (s) | S1 \|Δ\| (s) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Excluding LIRF NM-missing | 0.23 | 1.33 |
  | NM-present rows outside LIRF | 0.21 | 0.91 |
  | LIRF NM-missing (52 / 337 rows) | 124 | 82 |

  Bagged mixture predictions on a few day-scale LIRF convention records move by thousands of seconds between seeds. Example: row 200300302 (y = 87,598 s) is predicted at 16,148 s with seed 42 and 13,746 s with seed 43.
- **Seed averaging cannot fix this within budget.** The seed-to-seed shift of a k-seed average falls roughly as 1/√k, so S1's 3.50 s would need k ≈ 16 seeds (≈ 4 h per run, beyond CLASS-L).
- **The model code consumes the seed only through subsampling.** `src/prc/models/gbm.py` sets `deterministic=True` and `force_row_wise=True`.
  - `tests/test_models.py::test_lightgbm_without_subsampling_is_seed_invariant` shows that with `bagging_fraction` 1.0, `bagging_freq` 0 and `feature_fraction` 1.0, seeds 42 and 43 give identical predictions.
  - The Day 1/2 bagged configuration does not.
  - A 4-thread check on 20,000 synthetic rows gave identical predictions across seeds and across reruns (commit `f84fea3`).

## Mechanism

- **Removing subsampling** removes the model's dependence on the seed. The fitted model becomes a deterministic function of the fold's training data.
- **On bulk rows,** where E012 and E015 agreed to within 0.2–0.9 s, the no-subsampling model is expected to behave like H009, with a small systematic change from the loss of bagging's regularisation (either sign).
- **The mechanisms M1–M3 of H009 are unchanged,** because the inputs are unchanged. Their evidence is re-established against matched or bounded references (Falsification Criterion).
- **What this does and does not claim.** It does **not** claim better accuracy. It claims that the H009 model class, fitted deterministically, keeps its margin and mechanisms. Its predictions on the LIRF convention records are then **one fixed function of the data** rather than a seed draw.

## Proposed Change

LightGBM on **FS1** (`fs1`, unchanged since `cba278d`), with H009 v3's parameters except three:

| Parameter | H009 v3 | H013 |
|---|---|---|
| `bagging_fraction` | 0.8 | **1.0** |
| `bagging_freq` | 1 | **0** |
| `feature_fraction` | 0.9 | **1.0** |

- Unchanged: `objective` regression, `learning_rate` 0.05, `num_leaves` 255, `min_data_in_leaf` 100, `num_threads` 4, `num_boost_round` 1000, and the categorical defaults.
- There is no search. These three values are the unique "no subsampling" setting, not a tuned choice.

## Falsification Criterion

Every quantity comes from committed code (`scripts/compare.py`, `scripts/mechanism_check.py`, unchanged since `5ba9230`). H013 is falsified if **any** of the following holds:

1. **Promotion.** It fails frozen criteria 1–3 against E005 (`compare.py <H013> E005`).
2. **M1 (static structure), matched reference H014** (FS0, no subsampling):
   - **(a)** `mechanism_check.py <H013> <H014> NM_present_excl_LIRF` fails criterion 1 or criterion 2; **or**
   - **(b)** its NM-present bulk dRMSE (`mechanism_check.py <H013> <H014> NM_present`, `per_fold.*.delta_rmse_bulk`) is ≥ 0 on two or more development folds.
3. **M3 (convention).** Against **E013** (H010), `mechanism_check.py <H013> E013 LIRF_NM_missing` gives full dRMSE ≥ 0 on three or more of the five development folds.
   - E013 differs in `d_sched` **and** in subsampling.
   - The subsampling confound on this subgroup is bounded by the E015 − E012 shifts: 82–124 s on S1 and R3. The E012 − E013 margins were −314 to −2,493 s.
4. **M2 (anchor).** Against **E014** (H012), `mechanism_check.py <H013> E014 NM_present` fails criterion 1 or criterion 2.
   - Confound bound: E015 − E012 on NM-present rows outside LIRF was ≤ 0.91 s; the E012 − E014 margin was −51.3 s.

**Criterion 8 (the LIRF bulk trade)** is resolved only if clause 3 is not met **and** `NM_missing_LIRF.delta_rmse_bulk` against E005 is ≤ +7,000 s on every development fold. The bound is widened from H009's +6,500 s because E012 reached +6,390 s on S1 and a single deterministic draw can differ by the measured seed shifts. The widening is stated here, before the run.

**Criterion 6** is the frozen reproduction rule, unchanged.

## Alternative Explanations

1. **Bagging was regularising the day-scale predictions.** Without it, predictions on LIRF convention rows could become more extreme, worsening the bulk trade. Clause 1, the criterion 8 bound and `compare.py <H013> E012` expose this.
2. **An M1 pass could reflect the no-subsampling configuration interacting with the static keys** rather than static structure alone. The matched reference H014 holds the configuration fixed.
