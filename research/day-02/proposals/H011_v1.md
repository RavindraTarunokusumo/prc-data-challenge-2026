# Hypothesis H011: static structure without the anchor (LightGBM on FS1 minus deltas)

Part of the Day 2 batch X-D02-S01-0001. The design table and shared context are in `H009_v1.md`. **H011 is an ablation and diagnostic, never a promotion candidate.**

## Research Question

How much taxi-out signal do static and schedule keys carry with **no** NM/schedule time deltas, i.e. without the anchor? This is the brief's literal Day 2 question. It also serves as H009's M2 ablation (the anchor correction in the static-keys context).

## Observation

- E010 (LightGBM on FS0 without deltas) scored a development mean of 502.34, and E006 beat it by 124.5 s.
- FS0 without deltas holds only airport, airport × runway, wake, market, flight type, takeoff hour and weekday.
- EDA: against the airport median, stand removes 43 s of bulk RMSE on its own, operator 33 s, destination 32 s and aircraft type 29 s.

## Mechanism

M1 (H009) in isolation. Stand, type, operator, destination and the local scheduled time set a static expectation of taxi duration. Without the anchor, nothing indicates the individual flight's delay or hold.

## Proposed Change

LightGBM with H006's fixed parameters and seed 42, on **FS1_NO_DELTAS** (`src/prc/features.py: fs1_no_deltas`, commit `cba278d`).
- It is FS1 minus `d_aobt3`, `d_eobt1`, `d_sched` and `flt_missing`, i.e. exactly E010's FS0_NO_DELTAS plus the six FS1 keys.
- Two comparisons use it:
  - against **E010**: the Day 2 question (static keys, no anchor);
  - against **H009**: the M2 ablation.

## Controlled Variables

- Against E010: identical except the six added FS1 columns.
- Against H009: identical except the four deltas.

### Expected RMSE Direction

improve (against E010)

## Expected Affected Segments

- **Bulk (300–1,800 s) at every airport,** most at multi-runway, stand-heavy airports.
- **NM-missing rows at all airports.** FS0 without deltas gave them only airport, runway, hour and weekday, and FS1 adds stand, type, operator and destination.
- **Standing rule 8.** The H011 − E010 margin is not expected to concentrate on LIRF tail rows or NM-missing rows. Neither model contains `d_sched`, so the convention is out of reach of both. Expected share of the SSE change from LIRF NM-missing rows: < 0.3 in absolute value on each fold. The mechanism claimed is taxi duration.
- **S1.** H011 − E010 < 0 expected.

## Falsification Criterion

The Day 2 static-signal claim (without the anchor) is falsified if H011 against E010 fails frozen criterion 1 (mean dRMSE ≤ −1.0 s with q95 < 0), **or** has fewer than 3 WINs among the five development folds.

The M2 clause in H009's chain fails if H009 against H011 does not pass criteria 1–2.

## Standing Review Rules

1. **Tail attribution.** A bulk-dominated margin is expected (tail share < 0.5). There is no tail mechanism claim.
2. **Forward exposure.** Stand, operator and destination are regime-sensitive. Expected sign of H011 − E010 on S1c: < 0. On W1c: ≤ 0, and a TIE is possible (January-only training, more rare levels).
3. **Holdout use.** H is predicted only.
4. **Calendar-month identity.** There is no calendar-month key.
5. **Fold coverage.** All seven scored folds plus H. W1c fallback: none needed.
6. **Row concentration.** Reported. Without the anchor, the day-scale rows are expected to hurt both models equally.
7. **Subgroup disclosure.** Reported by `compare.py`.
8. **Recording convention.** Not engaged (see Expected Affected Segments).
