# Hypothesis H013 v2: LightGBM on FS1 with no random component (H009 without subsampling, binned from all rows)

**v2 replaces v1** (`H013_v1.md`, REVISE in X-D02-S01-0004). "Changes from v1" at the end maps the five required items.

Day 2 batch X-D02-S01-0005: **H013 v2** (candidate) and **H014 v2** (its matched M1 reference). H014 v1's ACCEPT lapsed by its own rule, because v2 changes the training configuration.

## Research Question

Does the H009 model keep its margin over the champion and its mechanisms when it is fitted by a training procedure with **no random component at all**? Such a procedure makes the frozen criterion 6 a determinism check, which the X-D02-S01-0004 ruling accepts.

## Observation

- **H009 v3 (E012)** was not falsified on any of its four clauses. Against E005 it gave −106.58 s, 7/7 WIN, and the criterion 8 objection is resolved.
- **Its reproduction (E015, seed 43) failed criterion 6:** |Δ| 1.73 s on R3 and 3.50 s on S1. H009 is INCONCLUSIVE.
- **Two random components differ between E012 and E015,** and they are not separated (`experiments/E015/analysis.md`, appended note):
  - the subsampling draws (`bagging_fraction` 0.8, `feature_fraction` 0.9);
  - the **bin-construction sample**. Above `bin_construct_sample_cnt` (default 200,000) training rows, LightGBM draws it from a seed derived from `seed`.
- **Every fold except W1c exceeds that default.** Training DEP rows, target-free count:

  | R1 | R2 | R3 | S1 | W1 | S1c | W1c | H |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1,387,414 | 1,571,364 | 1,757,038 | 1,537,475 | 1,611,189 | 1,005,519 | 153,706 | 1,919,370 |

- **Where the E015 − E012 shift lives** (`E015_vs_E012_mech_*.json`; both components together):

  | Population | R3 \|Δ\| (s) | S1 \|Δ\| (s) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Excluding LIRF NM-missing | 0.23 | 1.33 |
  | NM-present rows outside LIRF | 0.21 | 0.91 |

- **Committed evidence for determinism** (`tests/test_models.py::test_lightgbm_seed_invariance_above_bin_sample_threshold`, 260,000 synthetic training rows with continuous heavy-tailed features, 4 threads, the unchanged `gbm.lightgbm`):
  - no subsampling with the default bin sample: seeds 42 and 43 give **different** predictions;
  - no subsampling with `bin_construct_sample_cnt` 5,000,000: seeds 42 and 43 and a rerun give **identical** predictions.
  - The Advisor's check (400,000 rows) and the researcher's re-run of it agree.
  - `gbm.py` already sets `deterministic=True` and `force_row_wise=True`.

## Mechanism

- **No random component.** With no row or feature subsampling, and bins built from **every** training row (`bin_construct_sample_cnt` ≥ 1,919,370, the largest fold), the fitted model is a deterministic function of the fold's training data. The seed is not used.
- **The inputs are FS1, unchanged,** so mechanisms M1–M3 of H009 carry over. Their evidence is re-established against a matched reference (M1) or reused runs with an admissibility rule (M2, M3).
- **What this does and does not claim.** It does **not** claim better accuracy, nor that predictions on LIRF convention records are *stable* against data perturbation. It claims that the H009 model class, fitted deterministically, keeps its margin and mechanisms.
- **Standing disclosure.** E015's spread (R3 1.73 s, S1 3.50 s) is the measured sensitivity of this model class to its random components, and is recorded as a standing disclosure if H013 is promoted (X-D02-S01-0004, ruling 5).

## Proposed Change

LightGBM on **FS1** (`fs1`, unchanged since `cba278d`), with H009 v3's parameters except four:

| Parameter | H009 v3 | H013 v2 |
|---|---|---|
| `bagging_fraction` | 0.8 | **1.0** |
| `bagging_freq` | 1 | **0** |
| `feature_fraction` | 0.9 | **1.0** |
| `bin_construct_sample_cnt` | (default 200,000) | **5,000,000** (≥ every fold's training rows) |

- Unchanged: `objective` regression, `learning_rate` 0.05, `num_leaves` 255, `min_data_in_leaf` 100, `num_threads` 4, `num_boost_round` 1000, and the categorical defaults. Seed 42.
- There is no search. Each value is the unique setting that removes a random component, not a tuned choice.
- **Pinning `data_random_seed` was rejected:** under the criterion 6 ruling, fixing a random step independently of the seed does not satisfy the rule's intent.

### Reusing E013 and E014 (clauses 3 and 4): what is measured, and when the reuse is inadmissible

- **What is not measured.** E013 and E014 were fitted with subsampling and default binning. H013 has neither. No run measures what switching them off does to **E013 or E014**. The E015 − E012 shifts measure seed spread under the bagged configuration, not this configuration change, and they are **not** used as bounds.
- **What is measured.** The configuration change on the **candidate side**: H013 − E012 has the same features and a different configuration. Chain step 1 reports it as `mechanism_check.py <H013> E012 LIRF_NM_missing` and `mechanism_check.py <H013> E012 NM_present`. The same configuration change on the reference side is assumed to be of comparable size. That assumption is not tested.
- **Admissibility, per development fold f.** The reuse is inadmissible on f if the measured candidate-side shift is at least **half** the H009 margin that the clause relies on:

  | Clause | Shift statistic on f | Inadmissible if ≥ | R1 | R2 | R3 | S1 | W1 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 3 | \|`per_fold[f].delta_rmse_full`\| of `<H013> E012 LIRF_NM_missing` | ½ \|E012 − E013\| on `LIRF_NM_missing` | 544.3 | 157.1 | 1,246.5 | 988.1 | 1,237.6 |
  | 4 | \|`per_fold[f].delta_rmse_full`\| of `<H013> E012 NM_present` | ½ \|E012 − E014\| on `NM_present` | 16.6 | 18.3 | 29.6 | 21.9 | 41.9 |

  The margins are from `E012_vs_E013_mech_LIRF_NM_missing.json` (R1 −1,088.64, R2 −314.20, R3 −2,492.94, S1 −1,976.25, W1 −2,475.16) and `E012_vs_E014_mech_NM_present.json` (−33.17, −36.64, −59.10, −43.85, −83.76).
- **How an inadmissible fold is counted (conservative).**
  - **Clause 3:** an inadmissible development fold counts as a **failing** fold (as if full dRMSE ≥ 0). Clause 3 is met if failing and inadmissible folds together number ≥ 3.
  - **Clause 4:** criteria 1–2 pool all folds, so if **any** development fold is inadmissible, clause 4 **cannot be decided by reuse** and is treated as **met**. H013 is then not promotable on Day 2 without a new, separately reviewed matched M2 ablation.
