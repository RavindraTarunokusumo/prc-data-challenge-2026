## Research Question

How much taxi-out signal do row-own static keys (stand, aircraft type, operator, destination) and the local scheduled time carry beyond the Day 1 anchor features, on the rows where the anchor exists?

Can the resulting LightGBM replace the ridge champion, with its LIRF mechanism pre-registered (standing rule 8; C1/C2 of X-D01-S01-0004)?

## Mechanism

Three mechanisms, each with its own ablation and each judged **on the population where it can act**.

- **M1. Static taxi structure (P; taxi duration).**
  - Stand position fixes the taxi distance to each runway.
  - Aircraft type and operator affect taxi speed and pushback and start-up procedures.
  - Destination proxies flight length and type.
  - Local scheduled hour and weekday proxy the airport's diurnal and weekly demand in its own clock.
  - Trees combine these with `airport_runway` (stand × runway = the taxi route).
  - Ablation: H009 against **E006** on **NM-present rows**. There, both models already have exact `d_sched`, `d_aobt3` and `d_eobt1`, so the scheduled-time keys add **no new delay information**, only a P-labelled diurnal/weekly profile. The LIRF NM-missing subgroup is excluded by construction.
- **M2. Anchor correction (T; taxi duration).**
  - A nonlinear correction of `MVT − AOBT_3` (and `MVT − EOBT_1`) by airport × runway × delay regime.
  - Ablation: H009 against **H012** (FS1 minus `d_aobt3` and `d_eobt1`). This holds **`d_sched`, `flt_missing` and the static keys fixed**. It is judged on **NM-present rows**, because the anchor is null on NM-missing rows in both models.
- **M3. LIRF block-at-schedule recording convention (T; a recording convention, not taxi duration).**
  - For LIRF departures without NM data, the recorded block time is often SCHED_TIME, so the target is `d_sched`.
  - The L2-optimal prediction is a mixture, roughly p · `d_sched` + (1 − p) · (normal taxi time). It loses in the bulk and gains in the tail on that subgroup.
  - **FS1 carries the schedule delay by two routes:**
    - exact `d_sched`;
    - an hour-resolution proxy: `hour_utc` and `weekday` of takeoff (T) against `sched_hour_local` and `sched_weekday_local` (P). This gives the delay modulo 24 h to the hour, plus a day-crossing bit.
  - Ablation: H009 against **H010** (FS1 minus `d_sched`), which removes only the first route. **The M3 clause therefore tests what exact `d_sched` adds beyond the hour-resolution proxy.** A failed clause 3 would **not** show that the convention is absent. It would show that the proxy captures it.

## Proposed Change

**FS1 = FS0**, byte-identical and tested on real silver, **plus:**

| Feature | Type | Definition | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| `stand` | categorical | `STAND_mvt` (null → `NA`) | P* |
| `actype` | categorical | `AIRCRAFT_TYPE_mvt` (null → `UNK`) | P |
| `op_prefix` | categorical | first 3 characters of `FLIGHT_mvt` (null → `UNK`) | P |

\* **Stand.** The departure stand, recorded after the fact. It is assumed to be assigned before pushback, like runway (DATASET_AUDIT §6.2). The review found no winter-only or de-icing-like stand names.

† **`ades`** is **F** for diverted flights: 641 of 2,070 diversions, ~0.03 % of rows. These rows must be handled in the Days 5–7 causal-only variant.

**Rare-level collapse.** Levels of the four new categoricals with fewer than 100 **training** DEP rows in the fold become `__RARE__`. The collapse uses counts only, never the target. Unseen validation levels become `__RARE__` too.

**Model.** `lightgbm` (`src/prc/models/gbm.py`, unchanged). The H006 parameters are unchanged:
- `objective` regression, `learning_rate` 0.05, `num_leaves` 255, `min_data_in_leaf` 100, `feature_fraction` 0.9, `bagging_fraction` 0.8, `bagging_freq` 1, `num_threads` 4, `num_boost_round` 1000;

## Alternative Explanations

1. **Static gains are an NM-missing artifact.** Addressed by construction: M1 is judged on NM-present rows only.
2. **`op_prefix` and `ades` act as identifiers of LIRF convention rows.** For example, operators that file late would have their rows re-routed.
   - The relevant contrast is **H009 against E006** on the LIRF NM-missing subgroup (`mechanism_check.py <H009> E006 LIRF_NM_missing`, reported with row concentration). This corrects v1, which named H010.
   - A large change there, concentrated on a few operators, would support this explanation.
3. **Stand encodes the LFPG runway regime** (Aug–Nov). Exposed by the rule 2 twins and criterion 3 (pooled airport RMSE).
4. **On NM-present rows, M1 could come from the scheduled-time keys rather than the four static keys.** H011 v2 (four static keys, no scheduled time, no anchor) against E010 gives the static-keys-only reading in a different context. No clause depends on splitting M1 further.
