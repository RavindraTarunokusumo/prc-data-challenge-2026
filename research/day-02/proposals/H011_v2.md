# Hypothesis H011 v2: static keys without the anchor and without a schedule-delay route (LightGBM)

**v2 replaces v1** (`H011_v1.md`, REVISE in X-D02-S01-0001). Part of the Day 2 batch X-D02-S01-0002 (design table in `H009_v2.md`). **H011 is a diagnostic, never a promotion candidate.** It is no longer H009's M2 comparator; H012 took over that role.

## Research Question

How much taxi-out signal do the four static keys (stand, aircraft type, operator prefix, destination) carry when the model has **no** NM/schedule time deltas and **no** route to the schedule delay? This is the brief's Day 2 question in its cleanest form.

## Observation

- E010 (LightGBM on FS0 without deltas) scored a development mean of 502.34. Its inputs are airport, airport × runway, wake, market, flight type, and the takeoff hour and weekday.
- EDA (never-validation months), bulk RMSE reduction against the airport median: stand 43 s, operator 33 s, destination 32 s, aircraft type 29 s.

## Mechanism

M1 restricted to the four static keys. Stand fixes the taxi distance to each runway, aircraft type and operator affect taxi speed and procedures, and destination proxies flight length and type. Without the anchor or the schedule delay, nothing indicates the individual flight's delay or hold.

**Why the scheduled local time is excluded (the v1 defect).** E010 and this model both contain `hour_utc` and `weekday` of **takeoff** (T). Adding `sched_hour_local` and `sched_weekday_local` would create the hour-resolution schedule-delay proxy (review X-D02-S01-0001). That proxy would give the model a route to the LIRF block-at-schedule convention and to flight delay in general. v2 therefore adds **only** the four static keys.

## Proposed Change

LightGBM with H006's parameters and seed 42, on **FS1_STATIC_NO_DELTAS** (`src/prc/features.py: fs1_static_no_deltas`, commit `19ba509`).
- It is exactly E010's FS0_NO_DELTAS plus `stand`, `actype`, `op_prefix` and `ades`.
- `tests/test_features_fs1.py` asserts on real silver that the added columns are exactly these four and that the FS0_NO_DELTAS columns are byte-identical.
- There are 11 model features (v1 had 13).

## Controlled Variables

Identical to E010 (H008), except for the four added columns: folds, evaluation population, metric, silver, masking, seed 42, model code, hyperparameters. The rare collapse applies to the four new keys, fold-local and counts-only. E010 as reference is permitted by X-D02-S01-0001. It ran from a dirty working tree (`git_dirty_at_run`), and the reuse lapses if `uv.lock`, the model code, `fs0` or silver change before this run.

## Expected Affected Segments

- **The bulk (300–1,800 s) at every airport,** most at multi-runway, stand-heavy airports.
- **NM-missing rows at all airports.** Static keys replace `UNK`.
- **Standing rule 8.** Neither model has `d_sched`, `d_aobt3`, `d_eobt1` or the scheduled local time, so neither has a route to the schedule delay.
  - The static keys can still shift the prior on LIRF NM-missing rows, for example through operators whose rows are often convention records.
  - Pre-registered for that subgroup (H011 against E010):

    | Segment | Expectation |
    |---|---|
    | Tail | `share_of_sse_change_tail` within ±0.3 per fold |
    | Bulk | `delta_rmse_bulk` within ±300 s per fold |
    | S1 | Within the same bounds |

  - The mechanism claimed is taxi duration. The convention is not claimed.

## Falsification Criterion

The static-signal claim is falsified if H011 against E010, on rows **excluding the LIRF NM-missing subgroup**, fails frozen criterion 1 **or** has fewer than 3 WINs among the five development folds. Both come from `scripts/mechanism_check.py <H011> E010 excl_LIRF_NM_missing`: `criterion_1` and `fold_outcome`.

## Standing Review Rules

1. **Tail attribution.** A bulk-dominated margin is expected (tail share < 0.5). There is no tail mechanism claim.
2. **Forward exposure.** Expected sign of H011 − E010 on S1c: < 0. On W1c: ≤ 0, and a TIE is possible (January-only training, more `__RARE__`).
3. **Holdout use.** H is predicted only.
4. **Calendar-month identity.** There is no calendar-month key.
5. **Fold coverage.** All seven scored folds plus H. W1c fallback: none needed.
6. **Row concentration.** Reported.
7. **Subgroup disclosure.** Reported by `compare.py`.
8. **Recording convention.** Engaged only through the static prior on LIRF NM-missing rows. The expectations are above. There is no route to the schedule delay.
