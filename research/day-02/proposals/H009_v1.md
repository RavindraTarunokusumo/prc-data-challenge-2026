# Hypothesis H009: static structure on top of the anchor (LightGBM on FS1)

Day 2 batch (X-D02-S01-0001): **H009** (candidate), **H010** (convention ablation) and **H011** (static structure without the anchor). Together with Day 1's E006 and E010 they form this design:

| | FS0 base keys | + static keys (FS1) |
|---|---|---|
| no NM/schedule deltas | E010 (H008, Day 1) | **H011** |
| all deltas | E006 (H006, Day 1) | **H009** |

Every cell uses the H006 LightGBM configuration unchanged. Only the feature set varies.

## Research Question

How much taxi-out signal do row-own static keys carry beyond the Day 1 anchor features? The keys are stand, aircraft type, operator, destination and the local scheduled time, and the question is the brief's Day 2 theme ("how much signal exists without inferred operational state").

Can the resulting LightGBM model replace the ridge champion, with its LIRF mechanism **pre-registered** this time? Standing rule 8 and C1/C2 of X-D01-S01-0004 require that registration.

## Observation

1. **Descriptive EDA** (`research/day-02/eda/EDA_STATIC_TEMPORAL.md`). It uses only months that are never a validation month (fit Jan and Mar–Jun, evaluate Aug 2025).
   - Out-of-time bulk RMSE reduction against the airport median:
     - stand: 43 s;
     - stand × runway: 53 s;
     - operator prefix: 33 s;
     - destination: 32 s;
     - aircraft type: 29 s;
     - local scheduled hour: 16 s.
   - On the NM-present anchor residual, each key still removes 5–15 s.
   - No key has unseen levels in the ranking DEP rows.
2. **FS0 contains none of these keys.** Its only static inputs are airport, airport × runway, wake, market and flight type. For NM-missing rows, the last three are `UNK`.
   - Stand, flight number, destination and aircraft type are present on 93–99.9 % of NM-missing rows (EDA §4).
3. **E006 (LightGBM FS0) against E005 (ridge FS0)** (C1, and the rule 7 table reproduced by `scripts/compare.py`):
   - LightGBM is better by 31–40 s in the bulk on NM-present rows on every development fold.
   - Its full-population margin runs mostly through LIRF NM-missing rows, where it approximates the block-at-schedule recording convention via `d_sched`. The bulk dRMSE on those rows is +1,985 to +6,229 s; the tail gain is 0.40–1.04 of the SSE change.

## Mechanism

Three mechanisms, each pre-registered with its own ablation. H009 claims all three.

- **M1. Static taxi structure (P, new; taxi duration).**
  - Stand position fixes the taxi distance to each runway.
  - Aircraft type and operator affect taxi speed, pushback and start-up time, and gate procedures.
  - Destination proxies the flight's length and type (long-haul heavy aircraft, specific terminals).
  - Local scheduled hour proxies the diurnal demand pattern in the airport's own clock. The Day 1 H003 review found DST effects under the UTC key.
  - Trees combine these with `airport_runway`, so stand × runway interactions (the taxi route) are learnable.
  - Ablation: H009 against **E006** (identical except FS0).
- **M2. Anchor correction (T; taxi duration).**
  - This is the Day 1 H006 mechanism: a nonlinear correction of `MVT − AOBT_3` by airport × runway × delay regime.
  - Already supported by E006 against E010 (−124.5 s, all WIN). It is re-tested here in the static-keys context.
  - Ablation: H009 against **H011**.
- **M3. LIRF block-at-schedule recording convention (T; recording convention, not taxi duration).**
  - For LIRF departures without NM data, the recorded block time often equals SCHED_TIME. The target is then `d_sched`.
  - Admissible inputs cannot identify such rows. EDA §5: the convention-tail rate is 0 % for `d_sched` ≤ 1 h, 43 % for 1–2 h and 70 % above 2 h.
  - The L2-optimal prediction is therefore a mixture, approximately p · `d_sched` + (1 − p) · (normal taxi time).
  - That mixture necessarily **loses in the bulk** on the rows that are not convention records and **gains in the tail** on those that are.
  - The frozen objective is RMSE over all rows (DATA_POLICY §10), so this trade is the objective, not an artifact of it.
  - Ablation: H009 against **H010** (FS1 minus `d_sched`).

## Proposed Change

LightGBM on the new feature set **FS1** (`src/prc/features.py: fs1`, commit `cba278d`; tests in `tests/test_features_fs1.py`).

**FS1 = FS0**, column-for-column and byte-identical (tested on real silver, R1 and W1c), **plus:**

| Feature | Type | Definition | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| `stand` | categorical | `STAND_mvt` (null → `NA`) | P* |
| `actype` | categorical | `AIRCRAFT_TYPE_mvt` (null → `UNK`) | P |
| `op_prefix` | categorical | first 3 characters of `FLIGHT_mvt` (null → `UNK`); the ICAO operator prefix for most rows, and defined on NM-missing rows | P |
| `ades` | categorical | `ADES_mvt` (null → `UNK`) | P |
| `sched_hour_local` | numeric | hour of `SCHED_TIME_UTC_mvt` in the departure airport's IANA time zone | P |
| `sched_weekday_local` | numeric | weekday of the same local time | P |

\* **Stand**, like runway (DATASET_AUDIT §6.2), is recorded after the fact and assumed to be assigned before pushback. It is the departure stand, from which the aircraft pushes back, so this assumption is weaker for stand than for runway.

**Rare-level collapse.** Levels of the four new categoricals with fewer than 100 **training** DEP rows in the fold become `__RARE__`.
- The threshold is fixed a priori and equals LightGBM's default `min_data_per_group`.
- It uses row counts only, never the target.
- Unseen validation levels become `__RARE__` too.
- Rare shares on S1: stand 0.8 %, actype 0.1 %, op_prefix 1.6 %, ades 0.8 % of training rows.

**Not added, with reasons:**
- `stand_runway`: trees can form it from `stand` × `airport_runway`, and it would leave 3 % of EVAL rows unseen.
- NM operator: null on NM-missing rows, and redundant with `op_prefix`.
- Calendar month or day of year: no fold can validate them (standing rule 4, and validation months are never training months).
- Holidays: no admissible calendar source is listed in `config/network.yaml`.

**Model.** `lightgbm` (`src/prc/models/gbm.py`, unchanged). The fixed parameters are those of H006, unchanged: `objective` regression, `learning_rate` 0.05, `num_leaves` 255, `min_data_in_leaf` 100, `feature_fraction` 0.9, `bagging_fraction` 0.8, `bagging_freq` 1, `num_threads` 4, `num_boost_round` 1000.
- LightGBM's categorical defaults apply (`max_cat_threshold` 32, `cat_smooth` 10, `cat_l2` 10, `min_data_per_group` 100).
- There is no search and no early stopping. One configuration is trained per fold.

## Expected Affected Segments

**M1.** The bulk (300–1,800 s) of NM-present rows at every airport. The largest effect is expected at stand-heavy, multi-runway airports (EGLL, LFPG, EHAM, EDDF). NM-missing rows at the other nine airports also gain, because FS0 gave them `UNK` for wake, market and flight type, while FS1 gives them stand, type, operator and destination.

**M3 (standing rule 8)** on LIRF NM-missing rows, against E005:

|---|---|

The bulk gain on NM-present rows (−31 to −40 s for E006) is expected on every fold, and to be larger for H009.
2. **Forward exposure.** Stand, operator and destination are regime-sensitive keys.
   - Expected sign of dRMSE against E006 on S1c: < 0.
   - On W1c (trained on January alone, where more levels collapse to `__RARE__`): ≤ 0, and a TIE is possible.
   - Against E005: < 0 on both twins.

   - W1c fallback: none needed, because there are no history features.
   - The rare collapse is fold-local, so W1c (January training) collapses more levels. This is the intended fallback toward FS0 behaviour.
6. **Row concentration (B4).** I expect the day-scale LIRF row 183903219 (y = 131,167 s, no NM data) to dominate W1 and W1c against E005, as for E006. Reported, not excluded.
7. **Subgroup disclosure.** `scripts/compare.py` now reports rule 7 automatically: for every comparison, the NM status × LIRF shares and bulk dRMSE, with a sign-disagreement flag. It reproduces the X-D01-S01-0004 table for E006 against E005 exactly.

## Leakage Analysis

**Information labels (DATASET_AUDIT §6.2).** The six new features are **P**. They are row-own static or schedule fields, present in `ranking.parquet` for scored DEP rows. The FS0 labels are unchanged: `hour_utc`, `weekday`, `d_aobt3`, `d_eobt1` and `d_sched` are **T**, and the rest are P. There are no cross-row, F or cross-month inputs.

**Fitted quantities.** Rare-level vocabularies (counts only), category vocabularies and trees all come from the fold's training DEP rows. Validation `y` is null in the masked view (`tests/test_features_fs1.py`, `tests/test_masking.py`).

**Identifiers.** `FLIGHT_mvt` is a flight number, not an identifier excluded by §6.2 (that exclusion covers `MVT_ID_mvt` and `FLIGHT_ID_mvt`). Only its 3-character operator prefix is used, not the full flight number, which churns (21 % unseen in ranking).

## Alternative Explanations

1. **Static gains are an NM-missing artifact.** Gains on NM-missing rows could come from FS1 giving them any information at all, rather than from the stand/type mechanism. Rule 7 separates NM-present from NM-missing rows, and clause 2 is judged on NM-present rows.
2. **`op_prefix` and `ades` act as identifiers of the LIRF convention rows** (for example operators that file late). The H009 − H010 comparison on the LIRF NM-missing subgroup, reported with row concentration, would show this.
3. **Stand encodes the runway regime.** In the LFPG Aug–Nov regime, stand × runway could fit a regime absent from the test months. Rule 2 twins and the per-airport pooled RMSE (criterion 3) expose this.
