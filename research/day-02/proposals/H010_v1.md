# Hypothesis H010: convention ablation (LightGBM on FS1 minus `d_sched`)

Part of the Day 2 batch X-D02-S01-0001. The design table and shared context are in `H009_v1.md`. **H010 is an ablation and never a promotion candidate.**

## Research Question

Does H009's gain on LIRF NM-missing rows run through `d_sched`? This is the block-at-schedule recording-convention mechanism (M3, label T). It is the ablation X-D01-S01-0004 named as missing: nothing on Day 1 separated H006's NM-present correction from its NM-missing / `d_sched` behaviour.

## Observation

At LIRF, 83 % of tail rows are block-at-schedule records, for which y ≈ `d_sched` (X-D01-S01-0004 (c)). On LIRF NM-missing rows, E006's predictions correlate 0.67–0.95 with `d_sched`. EDA §5: among LIRF NM-missing rows, the convention-tail rate is 0 % for `d_sched` ≤ 1 h and 70 % above 2 h.

## Mechanism

Without `d_sched`, the model cannot scale its prediction with the time between schedule and takeoff. On NM-missing rows, the only remaining delta is `flt_missing` (`d_aobt3` and `d_eobt1` are null there). The model therefore falls back to a static prior per LIRF key cell:
- it **loses** the tail gain on convention rows;
- it **recovers** the bulk loss on non-convention rows.

On NM-present rows, `d_eobt1` and `d_aobt3` carry most of the delay information that `d_sched` would add. The change there should be small.

## Proposed Change

LightGBM with the same fixed parameters and seed as H009 and H006, on **FS1_NO_DSCHED** (`src/prc/features.py: fs1_no_dsched`, commit `cba278d`): FS1 minus the single column `d_sched`. Nothing else changes. `hour_utc` (takeoff hour, T) remains.

## Controlled Variables

Identical to H009 (folds, evaluation population, metric, silver, masking, seed 42, model code, hyperparameters, rare collapse). The only difference is one column: `d_sched`.

## Scientific Value

It supplies the missing Day 1 ablation. It separates the convention mechanism from the taxi-duration mechanisms and quantifies how much of the LightGBM margin is the LIRF convention. Days 5–7 need that quantity for the causal-only variant.

## Alternative Explanations

- **`d_sched` also carries genuine delay information** on NM-present rows (for example a late pushback correlated with congestion). That would show up as an NM-present bulk loss beyond +8 s.
- **`hour_utc` (takeoff hour) and `sched_hour_local` jointly encode part of `d_sched`**, because their difference is the delay modulo 24 h. H010 may therefore keep part of the convention signal, and the M3 effect measured here is a lower bound.
