# Hypothesis H012: anchor ablation with `d_sched` held fixed (LightGBM on FS1 minus `d_aobt3`, `d_eobt1`)

Part of the Day 2 batch X-D02-S01-0002 (design table in `H009_v2.md`). **H012 is H009's M2 ablation and never a promotion candidate.**

## Research Question

Does H009 gain from the NM anchor correction (M2: `MVT − AOBT_3` and `MVT − EOBT_1`) once `d_sched`, `flt_missing` and the static keys are held fixed? Review X-D02-S01-0001 required this contrast. H008 on Day 1 and H011 v1 removed `d_sched` together with the anchor, so they could not attribute a gain to the anchor.

## Observation

- **The audit's target proxies** (DATASET_AUDIT §6.3):

  | Proxy | RMSE (s) |
  |---|---|
  | `MVT − AOBT_3` | 385 |
  | `MVT − EOBT_1` | 677 |
  | `MVT − SCHED` | 2,413 |

- **Day 1 ablation.** E006 beat E010 (all four deltas removed) by 124.5 s. The share of that owed to the anchor rather than to `d_sched` is unknown.

## Mechanism

On NM-present rows, `d_aobt3` locates NM's off-block estimate relative to takeoff, and `d_eobt1` the flight-plan off-block. Their nonlinear correction by airport × runway × delay regime is M2.

Without them, the model still has `d_sched`. On NM-present rows, `d_sched` is the takeoff delay against schedule, a far noisier stand-in for the taxi interval, because it mixes gate delay into taxi.

On NM-missing rows, `d_aobt3` and `d_eobt1` are null in both H009 and H012. **M2 cannot act there**, so the M2 test is on NM-present rows.

## Proposed Change

LightGBM with H009's parameters, seed and folds, on **FS1_NO_ANCHOR** (`src/prc/features.py: fs1_no_anchor`, commit `19ba509`). It is FS1 minus exactly `{d_aobt3, d_eobt1}`, asserted on real silver (R1 and W1c) in `tests/test_features_fs1.py`. `d_sched`, `flt_missing`, the static keys and the time keys are kept.

## Controlled Variables

Identical to H009 v2 (folds, evaluation population, metric, silver, masking, seed 42, model code, hyperparameters, rare collapse), except for two columns.

## Scientific Value

It isolates the Day 1 anchor mechanism from the schedule delay for the first time. It quantifies how much of the model rests on NM's off-block estimate, which matters for the 1.5 % of January and July 2026 rows that lack it.

## Alternative Explanations

- **`d_eobt1` carries flight-plan delay rather than an anchor.** Removing it with `d_aobt3` mixes two NM quantities. Both are NM off-block times, and M2 is stated as their joint correction. No clause attributes the effect to one of them.
