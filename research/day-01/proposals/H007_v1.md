# Hypothesis H007: XGBoost baseline on FS0 (Tier 1)

## Research Question
Is the Tier 1 result robust to the boosting implementation (depth-wise XGBoost vs leaf-wise LightGBM)?

## Observation
Same as H006.

## Mechanism
Same as H006. Only the tree-growing algorithm and its regularisation differ.

## Proposed Change
XGBoost `hist`, L2 loss, native categoricals (partition-based splits), with the fixed a-priori hyperparameters listed below. No search and no early stopping.

Model: `xgboost` (`src/prc/models/`). Feature set: `FS0`.

Fixed parameters:
- `objective`: reg:squarederror
- `eta`: 0.05
- `max_depth`: 10
- `min_child_weight`: 10
- `subsample`: 0.8
- `colsample_bytree`: 0.9
- `max_cat_to_onehot`: 1
- `nthread`: 4
- `num_boost_round`: 1000

## Controlled Variables
The frozen folds, evaluation population, metric, promotion rule, silver layer (`data/manifests/silver_manifest.json`), masking protocol and seed 42 are the same for every Day 1 baseline (H001–H008).

FS0 (`src/prc/features.py`) is row-own columns only (DATASET_AUDIT §6.2 label: static):
- categoricals: `ADEP_mvt`, `airport_runway` (`ADEP_RUNWAY`), `WK_TBL_CAT_flt`, `MARKET_SEGMENT_flt`, `FLIGHT_TYPE_flt`;
- numerics: `hour_utc` and `weekday` of takeoff, `d_aobt3 = MVT − AOBT_3_flt`, `d_eobt1 = MVT − EOBT_1_flt`, `d_sched = MVT − SCHED_TIME_UTC_mvt` (seconds), `flt_missing`.

There are no cross-row features, no target statistics and no high-cardinality categoricals (stand, type, operator, destination), which are left for Days 2–4.

H007 does not pass frozen criteria 1–2 against the better of H004 and H005. Separately, a difference from H006 of more than 5 % in mean development RMSE would contradict the claim that FS0 signal, not the implementation, drives the result.
## Validation Plan
The frozen folds (`config/splits.yaml`, SPLITS v2) are used unchanged:
- **Scored:** development folds R1, R2, R3, S1, W1 and diagnostic twins S1c, W1c. The evaluation population is all DEP rows of each validation month.
- **H:** *predicted only* (training on Jan–Nov, December targets masked), so that the pre-registered phase-close comparison (`splits.yaml: phase_close`) needs no re-run. H is **not scored** by this experiment. Its only reader is `evaluate.holdout_compare` at phase close.

Reported: RMSE per fold, the mean over the five development folds and the frozen segment reports (airport, airport bulk < 3,600 s, month, traffic regime, wake, taxi band). Comparisons between hypotheses use `evaluate.compare`, i.e. the frozen paired-bootstrap promotion rule (criteria 1–3). Seed 42. The run goes through `scripts/run_experiment.py`: masked view only, resource supervision, metrics, predictions manifest and ledger.

Initial-champion rule (pre-registered, shared by H001–H008):
- **Incumbent and order.** H001 (global mean) is the incumbent. Candidates are then tested in this fixed order of increasing complexity: H002, H003, H005, H004, H006, H007. H008 is an ablation and never a candidate.
- **Promotion.** A candidate replaces the current champion only if it meets all three conditions:
  - it passes the frozen criteria 1–3 against the current champion (`evaluate.compare`);
  - its gate-allocated `reproduction` run (seed 43) meets the frozen reproduction rule;
  - its mechanism ablation, named below, supports its claim (criterion 4).
- **Result.** The champion at the end of the sequence is the initial champion.
- **Holdout at Day 1 close.** One access compares it with H001 on H. A LOSS reverts to H001.
## Required Ablation
H006 (same features, different implementation) and H008 (the FS0 ablation, shared). Criterion 4 is supported if H007 passes criteria 1–2 against H008.
## Standing Review Rules (SPLITS v2 review)
1. **Tail attribution.** Every comparison reports per-fold full vs bulk (y < 3,600 s) dRMSE and the tail share of the SSE change (`scripts/compare.py`). Pre-registered tail mechanism: very long taxi-outs (de-icing, runway queues, ground holds) happen largely before takeoff but after NM's recorded off-block, so `MVT − AOBT_3` reflects part of them. The anchor-based models may therefore gain in the ≥ 3,600 s band as well as in the bulk. I still expect most of the criterion-1 margin to come from the bulk (tail share of the SSE change < 0.5).
2. **Forward exposure.** As H006. Expected sign: S1c and W1c: dRMSE vs H004/H005 < 0.
3. **Holdout use.** H is predicted, never scored here. It is cited only through the Day 1 phase-close outcome.
4. **Calendar-month identity.** FS0 contains no calendar-month key (only takeoff hour and weekday).
5. **Fold coverage.** All seven scored folds plus H. W1c fallback: As H006.
