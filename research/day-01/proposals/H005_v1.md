# Hypothesis H005: Unfitted anchor proxy: takeoff − NM actual off-block (Tier 0)

## Research Question
How good is the raw admissible anchor `MVT − AOBT_3_flt` as a predictor on the frozen folds?

## Observation
The audit measured RMSE 384.9 s on all training rows (98.9 % coverage). `BLOCK − AOBT_3` has median +51 s and IQR −122…+183 s.

## Mechanism
`AOBT_3` is NM's actual off-block estimate, so the difference is a direct noisy measurement of the taxi-out.

## Proposed Change
Predict `d_aobt3` where it is available, otherwise the training-row airport median. No fitting beyond that fallback.

Model: `anchor_aobt3` (`src/prc/models/`). Feature set: `FS0`.

Fixed parameters:
- none

## Controlled Variables
The frozen folds, evaluation population, metric, silver layer (`data/manifests/silver_manifest.json`), masking protocol and seed 42 are the same for every Day 1 baseline (H001–H008).

FS0 (`src/prc/features.py`) is row-own columns only (DATASET_AUDIT §6.2 label: static):
- categoricals: `ADEP_mvt`, `airport_runway` (`ADEP_RUNWAY`), `WK_TBL_CAT_flt`, `MARKET_SEGMENT_flt`, `FLIGHT_TYPE_flt`;
- numerics: `hour_utc` and `weekday` of takeoff, `d_aobt3 = MVT − AOBT_3_flt`, `d_eobt1 = MVT − EOBT_1_flt`, `d_sched = MVT − SCHED_TIME_UTC_mvt` (seconds), `flt_missing`.

There are no cross-row features, no target statistics and no high-cardinality categoricals (stand, type, operator, destination), which are left for Days 2–4.

## Expected Result
Mean RMSE 360–420 s. It beats H001–H003 on every fold and may beat H004.
### Expected RMSE Direction
improve
## Expected Affected Segments
The median error is small, but the error tail is large (the audit's 1–99 % range of `BLOCK − AOBT_3` is −1,194…+803 s).
## Falsification Criterion
H005 fails to beat H003 on at least 3 of 4 folds. That would contradict the audit's anchor finding, which I would then have to re-examine.
## Validation Plan
Frozen development folds R1, R2, R3, S1 (`config/splits.yaml`), unchanged. The evaluation population is all DEP rows of each validation month. Primary metric: RMSE per fold and the mean of the four fold RMSEs, with the frozen segment reports (airport, month, traffic regime, wake, taxi band). The protected holdout H is **not** accessed. Seed 42. The run goes through `scripts/run_experiment.py`, which provides the masked view only, supervises resources and writes metrics, predictions manifest and ledger.

Initial-champion rule (pre-registered, shared by H001–H008): the initial champion is the Day 1 baseline with the lowest mean development RMSE. It must beat H001 on all four folds, a `reproduction` re-run through the gate must reproduce it within 1.0 s RMSE on every fold, and its mechanism claim must be supported by its ablation. Ties within 1.0 s go to the simpler model.
## Required Ablation
The fallback-only variant is H002 (airport median everywhere).
