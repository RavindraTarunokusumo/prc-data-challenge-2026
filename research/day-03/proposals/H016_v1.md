# Hypothesis H016 v1: FS2 deterministic LightGBM, unrouted (the R ablation of H015 and the all-rows congestion reference)

**Role.** H016 is a **reference and ablation, not a promotion candidate.** It is not reproduced, and it is never NEW in a holdout access.

## Research Question

1. **The R ablation.** What does routing the LIRF NM-missing subgroup to the champion's model change, isolated exactly? H016 is H015's LightGBM fit without the routing.
2. **All-rows congestion contrast (rule 11).** What does the congestion block do on all rows, convention subgroup included, against the matched reference E017?
3. **Rule 8 at July's rate.** With congestion inputs, what is the unrouted model's criterion 8 statistic on S1?

## Observation

See H015 (EDA, subgroup forward risk). The phase-close review forecast +6,400 to +7,800 s on S1 for a deterministic FS1-family candidate without a subgroup treatment.

## Mechanism

- **As H015's C.** On LIRF NM-missing rows, the model keeps the learned convention mixture (Day 2 M3, via `d_sched` and the hour-resolution proxy).
- **Congestion inputs may shift that mixture.** A convention record at LIRF has taxi = takeoff − schedule, so its "taxi interval" in the T counts is anchored at `SCHED`. The T counts over (SCHED, t_to) then grow with the schedule delay, which is the M3 signal in another form.
