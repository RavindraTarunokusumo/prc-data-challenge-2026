# Hypothesis H017 v1: P-labelled congestion only (FS2_P, deterministic LightGBM, unrouted)

Batch X-D03-S01-0001 (see `H015_v1.md`).

**Role.** H017 is a **reference for decomposing C into its P and T parts; it is not a promotion candidate.** It is not reproduced, and it is never NEW in a holdout access.

## Research Question

How much of the congestion effect is available **at the off-block proxy (P)**, and how much needs the row's own taxi interval (T)? The P part is the causal-only variant that Days 5–7 must report (DATASET_AUDIT §6.2).

## Observation

EDA (H015 Observation):
- **Residual after the airport median.** The P features reduce it by 0.9–9.3 s each; the largest is `cg_dep_taxiing` at 9.3 s.
- **Residual after the anchor.** They reduce it by only 0.3–1.7 s. The T features reduce it by 9.7–11.8 s.

## Mechanism

- The state at pushback (the queue, taxiing arrivals and recent throughput) sets the expected waiting time.
- Most of it is already reflected in the anchor delta `d_aobt3` of E017. That delta is itself label T, because it uses the row's takeoff.
- **Expectation.** The P part adds little beyond E017; the T part adds most of C.

## Proposed Change

LightGBM (`model: lightgbm`) on **FS2_P** (`features.fs2_p`: FS1 plus the 10 P congestion features), with E017's parameters exactly. Seed 42.

## Controlled Variables

As H016, with the five T features removed.

## Expected Result

**Noise-floor statement (Missing Control 4).** The P effect's expected size is **below the 6.0 s floor**, about three times the largest measured seed or procedure shift on this population (H015 "Noise scale").
- **It is therefore informational.** It is claimed as a mechanism only if its mean point estimate reaches −6.0 s and criteria 1–2 pass on the population.
- **If it is smaller,** the Day 3 record says "P effect not distinguishable from training noise", not "no P effect".

| **T on top of P:** H016 − H017 | Mean −6 to −20 s; WIN on ≥ 4 of 5 development folds |

### Expected RMSE Direction

neutral to improve (P, against E017); improve (T on top of P)

## Expected Affected Segments

- **P:** NM-missing rows at the nine non-LIRF airports, where no anchor exists, and peak hours at the queue-dominated hubs.
- **T:** the bulk at every airport.
