## Research Question

1. **C, congestion.** Does the reconstructed traffic state around a departure reduce error beyond FS1, the static and anchor structure, when the training procedure is held fixed? The state is the departures and arrivals on the airport and on its runway, at the off-block proxy and during the taxi.
2. **R, routing.** Can a Tier 1 congestion model become promotable under the frozen criteria without betting on the LIRF NM-missing recording convention? The Day 2 phase close named that bet as the primary expected failure mode of Day 3.

## Mechanism

**C (congestion).**
- Taxi-out time is service time plus waiting time. The waiting time is set by the departure queue and by crossing arrival traffic, and the literature models it as a queue at the runway (Idris et al.; Pujet, Delcaire & Feron; Simaiakis & Balakrishnan).
- The queue is reconstructed from admissible movement records:
  - **P state at the off-block proxy:** aircraft taxiing out and in, recent takeoffs, pushbacks and landings, and scheduled demand in the next 30 min;
  - **T state during the row's own taxi:** takeoffs from the airport and the runway, landings, the runway's previous-takeoff gap, and runway throughput in the 15 min before the row's takeoff.
- The T counts measure how many aircraft were served ahead of the row, and how many arrivals competed for the runway, while it taxied. A fixed-length taxi interval contains more takeoffs when the queue is longer.

**R (routing).**
- Rows of the LIRF NM-missing subgroup (`ADEP_mvt == LIRF` and `AOBT_3` missing; label P) take the prediction of **E005's model class**: H004's FS0 ridge (α 1.0, winsor 0.005–0.995), fitted on the same fold inside the same run.
- The candidate's predictions on that subgroup are therefore **identical to the champion's**. Its exposure to the convention, in any month and at any convention rate, is the champion's exposure: zero relative risk, by construction.
- **This is a structural choice, not a threshold** (ruling B). It is pre-registered here, before any Day 3 run.
- **Its rationale is the forward risk** (the Observation above): 276 July 2026 subgroup rows, 2.4 to 5.3 times any 2025 month, at an unknown convention rate within the 2025 range 0.35–0.83.
- **It does not rest on E006–E018 outcomes.** The expected magnitudes below cite them only as sizes.

## Falsification Criterion

Tools: `scripts/compare.py`, `scripts/mechanism_check.py` and `src/prc/attribution.py`, unchanged since `5ba9230`, plus the integrity check `scripts/route_check.py` (new, below). H015 is falsified if **any** of the following holds:

1. **Promotion.** It fails frozen criteria 1–3 against E005 (`compare.py <H015> E005`).
2. **C, against the matched reference E017** (deterministic FS1, the identical training procedure; ruling R).
   - The population is `excl_LIRF_NM_missing`. On it, H015's predictions are its FS2 LightGBM's and E017's are its FS1 LightGBM's, so the routing plays no part.
   - **(a)** `mechanism_check.py <H015> E017 excl_LIRF_NM_missing` fails criterion 1 or criterion 2 (twin rule included); **or**
   - **(b)** its mean dRMSE point estimate is **> −6.0 s**; **or**
   - **(c)** `mechanism_check.py <H015> E017 NM_present` gives `per_fold.*.delta_rmse_bulk` ≥ 0 on two or more development folds.
3. **R, integrity** (`route_check.py`). Either condition makes the run **INVALID** as a test of R:
   - H015's predictions on the LIRF NM-missing rows of any fold differ from E005's prediction files (max \|Δ\| > 1e-6 s); **or**
   - outside that subgroup they differ from H016's (max \|Δ\| > 0).

   R claims a structure, not an accuracy gain. Its accuracy cost on the development folds is disclosed, not a clause.
4. **Criterion 8.** H009 v3's statistic, unchanged: `NM_missing_LIRF.delta_rmse_bulk` against E005. It must be ≤ +6,500 s on every development fold. Expected 0.0. H015 makes no M3 claim: its subgroup predictions do not come from a learned mixture.

**Notes on the P features.**
- **"Not yet airborne at my t_off" is known at t_off.** It uses others' takeoff times only through the comparison t_to_j > t_off_i.
- **The self term is excluded without reading the row's own takeoff time:** the count subtracts 1 and clips at 0 (docstring of `congestion.py`).
- **The runway is P by assumption** (§6.2).
- **The label depends on the proxy.** For the 1.1 % of rows without `AOBT_3`, the P features are anchored at `EOBT_1` or `SCHED`, the same proxy order that §6.2 uses for labelling.

**Inputs.**
- The inputs are DEP takeoff times, runways, `AOBT_3`, `EOBT_1` and `SCHED` of rows of the same view, and ARR landing, in-block and scheduled times.
- **Another DEP row's block time or target is never used.** Invariance under masking is verified on real data and by a unit test.
- All the inputs are in the §6.2 admissible set for a ranking row (item 2), and all are present in validation and ranking alike.

**Cross-month.**
- Windows reach only months in the fold's view, and are at most 30 min long.
- SUBMIT_JAN sees 31 Dec 2025, which is training data. SUBMIT_JUL has no June 2026 context for the first 30 min of 1 July (0.008 % of rows are in a first UTC hour).
- S1's validation month ends before the embargoed August.

**Staleness (§6.5, declared from Day 3).**
- The congestion features depend on no target statistic and on no data outside the row's own month and adjacent context.
- The only staleness is the model's, as for every Tier 1 fit: 2025 training for July 2026.

**Routing and fits.** The routing key is P. Both fits use only the fold's training rows.

## Alternative Explanations

1. **The T counts partly re-encode `d_aobt3`,** since the count of takeoffs in (t_off, t_to) grows with the interval length.
   - The reference E017 already contains `d_aobt3`, so C measures what is added beyond the interval length.
   - H017 without the T group bounds how much comes from the state at pushback.
2. **C could come from better handling of the NM-missing rows at the other nine airports,** where no anchor exists, rather than from queueing.
   - Clause 2(c) requires the bulk gain on NM-present rows, where the anchor is present.
3. **The routing could look good or bad on 2025 folds for reasons unrelated to 2026.**
   - That is why R is not claimed as an accuracy mechanism, and why its dev-fold cost is disclosed rather than tested.
