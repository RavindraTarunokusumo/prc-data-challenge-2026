## Research Question

Does adding **this CatBoost configuration** (H021) to the routed LightGBM (E029) in a fixed equal-weight average reduce error, through errors that are imperfectly correlated? Does the average beat the champion while keeping the D3-C2 treatment?
- **What is exact:** H023 − E029 = ½ (H021 − E029) at the prediction level.
- **What is not isolated:** H021 differs from E029 in learner, tree shape, feature set (raw against collapsed levels), categorical representation and random streams. **A complementarity gain is attributed to this CatBoost configuration as a whole, not to "using the same keys differently".**
- **Named, not run:** the equal-weight blend of E029 and H022 would attribute any gain to the categorical statistics.

## Mechanism

- **Averaging two predictors reduces error variance by their decorrelated part.** For error vectors with RMSE r₁ and r₂ and correlation ρ, the 50/50 blend's MSE is ¼r₁² + ¼r₂² + ½ρr₁r₂. It is below the better component only when ρ is low enough relative to the gap between r₁ and r₂.
- **The two learners differ in several ways at once** that should decorrelate their errors on normal taxis. Which one carries a gain is not tested:
  - tree shape (leaf-wise 255 leaves against symmetric depth 8);
  - categorical representation (gradient-sorted splits on collapsed levels against CTRs on raw levels and combinations);
  - row and split randomness (deterministic against Bayesian bootstrap and random strength).
- **On the tail they will not decorrelate.** Both miss the same day-scale records, so the gain must come from bulk rows.
- **Routing and the D3-C2 treatment are held in both components.** On the laptop, the CatBoost path's ridge input (FS2_RAW) is bit-identical to the LightGBM path's (FS2) (`research/day-05/eda/ridge_paths.json`). So the routed rows should be E029's in both, and in the blend. This is checked through H021's integrity clause.
