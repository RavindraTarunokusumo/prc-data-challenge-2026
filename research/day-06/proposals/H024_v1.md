## Research Question

How much of E031's categorical-statistics advantage, and of the champion's blend gain, survives when CatBoost builds **per-key** statistics only (no combinations of categorical keys)?

## Observation

- E031 (CTRs Borders/FeatureFreq/FloatTargetMeanValue, `max_ctr_complexity` 4) beats E030 (integer codes) by −4.91 s on `NM_present_excl_LIRF`.
- E031 takes 1,523 s (CLASS-L), E030 225 s: most of E031's cost is the CTRs, and at complexity 4 most CTR features are combinations.
- No run separates per-key statistics from combinations.

## Mechanism

At complexity 1, CatBoost still computes the three CTR types for each raw categorical key (FS2_RAW: airport, runway, stand, aircraft type, operator, destination and the other FS2 categoricals), but never for products of keys. If the useful statistics are the marginal per-key ones (e.g. stand or operator mean taxi time), complexity 1 keeps nearly all of E031's advantage; if the useful ones are interactions (airport × runway × hour-bucket style), it loses them.

## Proposed Change

`routed_catboost`, FS2_RAW, E031's configuration (H021 v3) with **one change: `max_ctr_complexity: 1`**. `combinations_ctr` is left as in E031 for byte-equal config text; at complexity 1 CatBoost builds no combinations, so it is inert (the resolved parameters are recorded in `resolved_params.json` and checked). Seed 42 (E031's). All 8 folds. Config:

```yaml
model: routed_catboost
feature_set: FS2_RAW
params: {loss_function: RMSE, task_type: GPU, devices: "0", depth: 8, iterations: 1000,
  learning_rate: 0.08, border_count: 254, l2_leaf_reg: 3, boosting_type: Plain,
  simple_ctr: [Borders, FeatureFreq, FloatTargetMeanValue],
  combinations_ctr: [Borders, FeatureFreq, FloatTargetMeanValue], max_ctr_complexity: 1,
  gpu_ram_part: 0.4, thread_count: 4, cat_mode: ctr,
  route_ridge_params: {alpha: 1.0, winsor: [0.005, 0.995]}, route_train_exclude: true}
folds: [R1, R2, R3, S1, W1, S1c, W1c, H]
seed: 42
job_class: CLASS-M
```
