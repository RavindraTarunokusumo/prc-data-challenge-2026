# Hypothesis H026 v1: equal-weight blend of E029 and the integer-codes CatBoost (E030); ladder rung B (control, not a candidate)

Batch X-D06-S01-0001. The claim under attack, the ladder, the readings and their thresholds, the noise reference, the batch status (no candidate, no holdout access), the shared validation plan, the tools freeze, the run window and order, the leakage analysis and the literature are in **H024 §Batch** (`research/day-06/proposals/H024_v1.md`) and apply here unchanged.

## Research Question

Does the champion's blend gain need CatBoost's categorical statistics at all, or does CatBoost's learner (symmetric trees, ordered boosting, Bayesian bootstrap) supply it with the categoricals as integer codes? Rung B of the ladder: the control named and not run in H023 v3 (revision 6).

## Observation

- E030 (codes) is worse than E029 on all rows by +4.05 s (LOSS on 5 of 7 folds), and E031 beats E030 by −4.91 s on `NM_present_excl_LIRF` within CatBoost.
- The blend's gain depends on the second half's accuracy *and* its decorrelation from E029. E030 is less accurate than E031 but differs from E029 in the same learner respects.

## Mechanism

Averaging reduces error by the ambiguity term (Krogh & Vedelsby 1995). If most of the CatBoost half's ambiguity with E029 comes from tree shape and boosting scheme, E030 supplies most of it despite its accuracy deficit. If it comes from the statistics, E030 does not.
