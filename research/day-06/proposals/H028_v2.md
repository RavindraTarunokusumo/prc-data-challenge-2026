## Research Question

What does generic averaging reach next to E029, with a perturbation twin at its measured disagreement? Rung A of the ladder. If the twin's blend keeps most of the −3.96 s (the bounded "carries" reading), E033's margin needs no CatBoost, and E033 gets that disclosure. If it does not, G(H028) is recorded as the generic-averaging floor at that disagreement, and **nothing is concluded about learner families**: rung A replaces the whole CatBoost half (learner, raw keys, statistics, hyperparameters), and its twin is close to E029 by design.

## Observation

E029 and E031's residual correlation is 0.94–0.99 on all rows and 0.84–0.92 on bulk rows. The blend's gain has never been compared with a same-family average.

## Mechanism

As H027: subsampling-perturbed copies of one learner reduce variance by their ambiguity. If E033's gain is variance reduction of this kind, rung A recovers it.
