## Research Question

What does a same-family, similar-accuracy, partly decorrelated second model look like next to E029? H027 builds it; H028 blends it.

## Observation

E029 is deterministic (`feature_fraction` 1.0, `bagging_fraction` 1.0, `bagging_freq` 0, `deterministic=True`): a seed change alone reproduces it, so it cannot serve as its own averaging partner. Row and feature subsampling is the standard way to get decorrelated copies of one learner (Breiman 1996).

## Mechanism

Bagging and feature subsampling perturb split choices, which decorrelates errors on rows where several splits are near-tied, while accuracy stays close to the full model. It is the averaging-only control: same features, same learner, same tree shape, same routing.

## Proposed Change

`routed_lightgbm`, FS2, E029's configuration with **four keys changed, one perturbation**: `feature_fraction: 0.8`, `bagging_fraction: 0.8`, `bagging_freq: 1`, and **seed 42** (the frozen `primary_seed`; E029, a reproduction, used 43). The seed now drives the subsampling. The 0.8 values are **the researcher's a-priori choice** (a common moderate subsampling level), fixed before any run; nothing is tuned. E029's own seed is inert (no subsampling; every training fold is below `bin_construct_sample_cnt`), and the routed ridge's rows are identical across seeds (`route_check_E030.json`, `route_check_E031.json`).

```yaml
model: routed_lightgbm
feature_set: FS2
params: {objective: regression, learning_rate: 0.05, num_leaves: 255, min_data_in_leaf: 100,
  feature_fraction: 0.8, bagging_fraction: 0.8, bagging_freq: 1,
  bin_construct_sample_cnt: 5000000, num_threads: 4, num_boost_round: 1000,
  route_train_exclude: true, route_ridge_params: {alpha: 1.0, winsor: [0.005, 0.995]}}
folds: [R1, R2, R3, S1, W1, S1c, W1c, H]
seed: 42
job_class: CLASS-M
```
