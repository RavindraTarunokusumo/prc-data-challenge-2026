## Research Question

How large is the GPU refit spread of E031's configuration at the seed Day 7 will use, and is a seed-42 refit byte-identical?

## Observation

E032 (seed 43) moved R1 by +1.06 s and W1 by −0.73 s against E031. DAY_SUMMARY §6.5 states an identical re-fit differs, but no full 8-fold seed-42 refit exists.

## Mechanism

CatBoost's GPU histogram and CTR computations use atomic floating-point reductions whose order varies between runs, so split scores near ties can change; a changed early split propagates through 1,000 trees.

## Proposed Change

`routed_catboost`, E031's config unchanged (H021 v3; `experiments/E031/config.yaml`), seed 42, all 8 folds:

```yaml
model: routed_catboost
feature_set: FS2_RAW
params: {loss_function: RMSE, task_type: GPU, devices: "0", depth: 8, iterations: 1000,
  learning_rate: 0.08, border_count: 254, l2_leaf_reg: 3, boosting_type: Plain,
  simple_ctr: [Borders, FeatureFreq, FloatTargetMeanValue],
  combinations_ctr: [Borders, FeatureFreq, FloatTargetMeanValue], max_ctr_complexity: 4,
  gpu_ram_part: 0.4, thread_count: 4, cat_mode: ctr,
```
