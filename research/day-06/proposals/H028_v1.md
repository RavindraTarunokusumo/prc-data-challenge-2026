# Hypothesis H028 v1: equal-weight blend of E029 and its subsampled LightGBM twin (H027); ladder rung A (control, not a candidate)

Batch X-D06-S01-0001. The claim under attack, the ladder, the readings and their thresholds, the noise reference, the batch status (no candidate, no holdout access), the shared validation plan, the tools freeze, the run window and order, the leakage analysis and the literature are in **H024 §Batch** (`research/day-06/proposals/H024_v1.md`) and apply here unchanged.

## Research Question

Is the champion's blend gain generic averaging? If a same-family twin of E029 gives most of the −3.96 s, "a second learner family adds signal" (Day 5 findings 2–3) is not the mechanism. Rung A of the ladder.

## Observation

E029 and E031's residual correlation is 0.94–0.99 on all rows and 0.84–0.92 on bulk rows. The blend's gain has never been compared with a same-family average.

## Mechanism

As H027: subsampling-perturbed copies of one learner reduce variance by their ambiguity. If E033's gain is variance reduction of this kind, rung A recovers it.

## Proposed Change

`blend`, components [E029, **H027's E### (E038)**], weights [0.5, 0.5] fixed a priori:

```yaml
model: blend
feature_set: FS0
params: {components: [E029, E038], weights: [0.5, 0.5]}
folds: [R1, R2, R3, S1, W1, S1c, W1c, H]
```
