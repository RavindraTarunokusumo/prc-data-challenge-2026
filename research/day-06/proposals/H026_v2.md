## Research Question

Does the champion's blend gain need CatBoost's categorical statistics at all, or does CatBoost's learner (oblivious trees, plain boosting, Bayesian bootstrap; `boosting_type: Plain`, `bootstrap_type: Bayesian`) supply it with the categoricals as FS2_RAW integer codes? Rung B of the ladder: the control named and not run in H023 v3 (revision 6). **The question is asked at equal weight** (E033's construction): a fixed 0.5 weight penalises a less accurate second half more than its best weight would, so a LOSS means "at E033's construction, the codes CatBoost is no substitute", including its weaker accuracy.

## Observation

- E030 (codes) is worse than E029 on all rows by +4.05 s (LOSS on 5 of 7 folds), and E031 beats E030 by −4.91 s on `NM_present_excl_LIRF` within CatBoost.
- The blend's gain depends on the second half's accuracy *and* its decorrelation from E029. E030 is less accurate than E031 but differs from E029 in the same learner respects.

## Mechanism

Averaging reduces error by the ambiguity term (Krogh & Vedelsby 1995). If most of the CatBoost half's ambiguity with E029 comes from oblivious trees, plain boosting with Bayesian bootstrap and the different split search on codes, E030 supplies most of it despite its accuracy deficit. If it comes from the statistics, E030 does not.
