### Purpose

Day 7 is final synthesis, submission and freeze (brief §3). The champion is **E033** (H023 v3): a fixed 0.5/0.5 blend of E029 (routed LightGBM, FS2, `route_train_exclude`) and E031 (routed CatBoost GPU, FS2_RAW, CTRs at complexity 4). **Not a candidate and not a hypothesis test.** Final folds have no truth: nothing is scored, nothing is promoted, the champion does not change, no ledger decision is made (role in `notes`). The batch answers one operational question: *does the champion's procedure, run on the final folds, produce a complete, internally consistent submission?* It is the procedure STATE already names ("SUBMIT predictions will be a further draw, and SUBMIT trains on 12 months"). Rule 10 is not engaged: the configurations are not re-submitted as candidates; they are the champion's own SUBMIT fit.
